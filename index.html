How Does It Work? A Simple Explanation

Step 1: Looking at Details

Just like how you notice edges and shapes first, our AI starts by detecting basic features like edges and corners in security footage.

Step 2: Combining Features

These basic features are combined to recognize more complex patterns, like the shape of objects or movement patterns.

Step 3: Making Decisions

Finally, the system decides if what it sees is a potential threat, just like how you would recognize dangerous situations.

After purchase, our certified technicians will visit your location to install and configure GuardiansafeAI on your existing security infrastructure. This includes setting up both desktop monitoring systems and mobile alert applications for designated personnel.
